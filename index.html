Tzeriz Consultant was founded in 2023 by a lawyer who saw that the decisions that matter most to a company or a family rarely fall within a single discipline.

How we work

Analyse, advise, operate, communicate

Our role does not end with a recommendation. We stay with the client through execution, and we account for the result.

  1. Step I

    Analyse

    We start with the facts: the documents, the figures, the people involved and the risks. No advice is given before the file is understood.

  2. Step II

    Advise

    We set out the options plainly, with their consequences, and recommend one. Our advice is written, reasoned and signed.

  3. Step III

    Operate

    Where the client wishes, we carry out the decision: we negotiate, coordinate other advisers and run the matter to completion.

  4. Step IV

    Communicate

    We report regularly and in writing. We also present our clients and their projects to the banks, investors and partners they need to convince.
