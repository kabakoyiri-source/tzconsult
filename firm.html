The Firm

One adviser for the whole file

Tzeriz Consultant is an independent advisory group led by a lawyer. Since 2023 we have advised, represented and managed the affairs of companies, private clients and professionals.

Our story

Why the firm exists

Too often, clients pass the same file from lawyer to banker to estate agent to accountant, each adviser seeing one part of it and none answering for the outcome. Tzeriz Consultant was set up in 2023 to work differently.

We take on fewer clients and go further with each of them. We study the legal position, the property and the numbers together. We give a clear recommendation. And when the client wants it, we stay on to carry it out, as their representative and, in some cases, as the manager of their affairs.

Our role

Adviser, representative, manager

Most of our work begins as advice. Over time, many clients ask us to take on more.

  1. I

    As adviser

    We analyse a situation and recommend a course of action.

  2. II

    As representative

    We act in the client's name with counterparties, banks, investors and authorities, under a written mandate.

  3. III

    As manager

    We look after the ongoing affairs of several companies and private clients: their contracts, their assets, their advisers and their reporting.

In each role, our duty is the same: to protect the client's interests and to tell them the truth about their position.

Leadership

A lawyer at the head of the firm

The firm is led by its founding partner, a lawyer by training, who remains personally responsible for every mandate we accept.

That training shapes how we work. Every recommendation is grounded in the documents and in the law that applies to them. Every commitment we make for a client is one we would be prepared to defend. And every client knows who is in charge of their file.

Around the founding partner, the firm draws on a close network of specialists in tax, notarial practice, banking and property, brought in matter by matter and coordinated by us.

Our principles

What clients can expect of us

These are the standards we hold ourselves to, and the ones our clients can hold us to.

I

Discretion

Our clients' affairs stay between them and us. We work under confidentiality agreements as a matter of course and share information only with their consent.

II

Independence

Our advice is given in the client's interest alone. Any potential conflict of interest is disclosed before we accept a mandate.

III

Rigour

We read the documents, check the figures and verify the people. Our advice is written down and can be relied on.

IV

Availability

Each client has direct access to the partner in charge, and we aim to reply within one working day.

Who we act for

Our clients

We act for clients who value a long relationship with an adviser who knows their affairs in detail.

01

Companies

Owner-managed businesses, holding companies and groups with operations in several countries. We advise their directors and shareholders, and for some of them we manage day-to-day legal and administrative affairs.

02

Private clients

Entrepreneurs, families and private investors. We look after their property, their investments and their projects, and represent them when they cannot or prefer not to attend in person.

03

Professional clients

Fund managers, developers, introducers and other professionals who need a reliable counterpart to represent them, review a transaction or manage a relationship.
