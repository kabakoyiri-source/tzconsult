Business advisory

We advise owners, directors and shareholders on the legal and strategic questions that shape a company.

  • Shareholder and partnership agreements

  • Acquisitions and disposals
  • Joint ventures and partnerships
  • Corporate governance
  • Disputes and pre-litigation strategy

Real estate

We act for investors, developers and private owners on residential, commercial and development property.

We review the title, the planning position and the numbers before a client commits. We negotiate the terms, coordinate notaries, lenders and agents, and follow the transaction through to completion.   • Development and land projects
  • Legal and technical due diligence
  • Title and planning review
  • Leases and occupational agreements
  • Property financing
  • Asset and portfolio management
  • Cross-border investment

Management of affairs

Managing affairs on our clients' behalf

For several companies and private clients, we go one step further and manage their affairs on an ongoing basis.

We keep their contracts, assets and deadlines in order, supervise the other advisers who work for them and handle their correspondence with third parties. At agreed intervals, they receive a written report on what has been done, what is pending and what needs their decision.

This arrangement suits clients who are often travelling, who hold assets in several countries, or who simply prefer to rely on one trusted firm.

Engagement

How a mandate works

Every engagement follows the same four stages, so that clients know what to expect and what it will cost before work begins.

  1. First meeting

    We listen to the situation, review the essential documents and tell you frankly whether and how we can help.

  2. Engagement letter

    We confirm in writing the scope of our work, the powers you give us, our fees and the confidentiality that applies.

  3. Execution

    We carry out the work agreed: analysis, advice, negotiation or management, with you informed at every stage.

  4. Reporting

    We report in writing on the outcome and, for ongoing mandates, at regular intervals agreed with you.
